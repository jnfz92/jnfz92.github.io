Los cuatro tiempos de pasado

TiempoUso principalEjemplo
Past SimpleAcciones terminadas en un momento pasadoI visited London last year.
Visité Londres el año pasado.
Past ContinuousAcciones en progreso en un momento del pasadoI was studying at 8 p.m.
Estaba estudiando a las ocho.
Past PerfectUna acción anterior a otra acción pasadaI had left before he arrived.
Me había ido antes de que él llegara.
Past Perfect ContinuousDuración de una actividad anterior a otro momento pasadoI had been studying for two hours.
Llevaba dos horas estudiando.

1. Past Simple

Usamos el Past Simple para acciones terminadas en un tiempo pasado que ya ha acabado.

Estructura

TipoEstructuraEjemplo
AfirmativaSubject + past formShe worked yesterday.
NegativaSubject + did not + base verbShe didn't work yesterday.
PreguntaDid + subject + base verb?Did she work yesterday?
  • Acción terminada: I called him last night.
  • Secuencia: I got up, had breakfast and left home.
  • Hábito pasado: We played football every weekend.
Marcadores frecuentes: yesterday, last week, ago, in 2024, when I was a child.

2. Past Continuous

El Past Continuous describe una acción que estaba en progreso en un momento del pasado.

Estructura

TipoEstructuraEjemplo
AfirmativaSubject + was/were + verb-ingI was reading.
NegativaSubject + wasn't/weren't + verb-ingThey weren't sleeping.
PreguntaWas/Were + subject + verb-ing?Were you studying?
  • Acción en progreso: At 9 p.m., I was watching TV.
  • Acción interrumpida: I was sleeping when the phone rang.
  • Dos acciones simultáneas: While I was cooking, she was setting the table.
When vs. while: when suele introducir la acción puntual; while, la acción en progreso.

3. Past Perfect

Usamos el Past Perfect para dejar claro qué acción ocurrió primero cuando hablamos de dos momentos en el pasado.

Estructura

TipoEstructuraEjemplo
AfirmativaSubject + had + past participleThey had left.
NegativaSubject + hadn't + past participleThey hadn't left.
PreguntaHad + subject + past participle?Had they left?
When I arrived, the film had started.
Cuando llegué, la película ya había empezado.

La película empezó primero; después llegué yo.

4. Past Perfect Continuous

El Past Perfect Continuous destaca cuánto tiempo llevaba ocurriendo una actividad antes de otro momento pasado.

Estructura

TipoEstructuraEjemplo
AfirmativaSubject + had been + verb-ingShe had been working all day.
NegativaSubject + hadn't been + verb-ingShe hadn't been sleeping well.
PreguntaHad + subject + been + verb-ing?Had you been waiting long?
  • Duración: He had been studying for three hours before dinner.
  • Causa de un resultado pasado: She was tired because she had been running.

Cómo elegir el tiempo correcto

Pregunta mentalTiempoEjemplo
¿La acción terminó?Past SimpleI finished at six.
¿Estaba en progreso?Past ContinuousI was working at six.
¿Ocurrió antes de otra acción pasada?Past PerfectI had finished before she arrived.
¿Importa cuánto tiempo llevaba ocurriendo?Past Perfect ContinuousI had been working for five hours.

Past Simple vs Past Continuous

I cooked dinner. → acción terminada.
I was cooking dinner when he called. → acción en progreso interrumpida.

Past Perfect vs Past Perfect Continuous

She had written five emails. → importa el resultado.
She had been writing emails all morning. → importa la actividad o duración.

Errores frecuentes

  • ❌ Did you went? ✅ Did you go?
  • ❌ I was study. ✅ I was studying.
  • ❌ When I arrived, he already left. ✅ When I arrived, he had already left.
  • ❌ She had been known him for years. ✅ She had known him for years. (know es stative).

Ejercicios

  1. Yesterday I ________ (go) to the cinema.
  2. At 10 p.m. we ________ (watch) a film.
  3. When I arrived, they ________ already ________ (eat).
  4. She was tired because she ________ (work) all day.
  5. While I ________ (drive), it started to rain.
  6. He ________ (not see) the message yesterday.
  7. By the time the police arrived, the thief ________ (leave).
  8. How long ________ you ________ (wait) before the bus came?

Soluciones

  1. went
  2. were watching
  3. had already eaten
  4. had been working
  5. was driving
  6. didn't see
  7. had left
  8. had / been waiting
