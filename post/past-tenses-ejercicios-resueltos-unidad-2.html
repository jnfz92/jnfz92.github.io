1. The Mystery of Kaspar Hauser · Solución

Completa el texto con el pasado simple de: be, begin, come, drink, eat, explain, feed, find, get, give, go, have, know, learn, meet, read, seem, speak, spread, write.

The mystery of Kaspar Hauser began in Nuremberg, Germany, about 200 years ago. One morning, the people of the town found a young man standing alone in the square. He was holding a piece of paper in his hand. The paper read only that he was the son of a soldier. Kaspar knew how to say a few words and when given a paper and pencil he wrote his name, but he seemed completely ignorant about everyday life. At first he ate only bread and drank only water, but he gradually got used to ordinary meals. He also learnt to talk properly.

The real truth about his birth remains a mystery, but it is probable that his father kept him in one small room for the whole of his early life. He fed him on bread and gave him water to drink. Kaspar never went out, he never spoke to anyone or met other children. In spite of his extraordinary childhood, Kaspar was not stupid. He read books and had discussions with teachers and philosophers. News about Kaspar spread through Europe and visitors came from abroad to meet him.

2. Past Simple or Past Continuous

1. My parents ___ (get) to know each other when they ___ (study) at university.

Respuesta: got / were studying

Got indica un hecho; were studying, una situación en desarrollo.

2. Doctor Fisher ___ (travel) widely as a young man and ___ (always keep) a diary.

Respuesta: travelled / always kept

Dos hábitos pasados: Past Simple. Traveled es la variante estadounidense.

3. I ___ (see) my brother and his friend when I ___ (wait) for the bus, but they ___ (not see) me.

Respuesta: saw / was waiting / didn't see

La espera estaba en curso; ver y no ver son hechos puntuales.

4. Lily ___ (fill) in the application form and ___ (give) it to the receptionist.

Respuesta: filled / gave

Dos acciones consecutivas y terminadas.

5. While I ___ (work) in Rome, I ___ (meet) a girl who ___ (look) just like your sister.

Respuesta: was working / met / looked

Contexto en desarrollo, encuentro puntual y descripción.

6. Simon ___ (buy) a new laptop because his old one ___ (always crash).

Respuesta: bought / was always crashing

Was always crashing destaca una repetición molesta.

7. I ___ (miss) your text because my phone ___ (charge) in another room.

Respuesta: missed / was charging

Hecho puntual y situación en progreso.

8. Anna's feeling depressed because she ___ (hope) for a pay rise last week, but she ___ (not get) one.

Respuesta: was hoping / didn't get

Expectativa durante un periodo y resultado negativo. Hoped también es posible según el matiz.

3. Choose the correct form

My granny is 93 and she's come to live with us at our house. We're all pleased because we love having her near us. She's a very independent person and until this year, she refused to move to the flat on our ground floor. But last month she suddenly changed her mind and I asked her why. She explained that for years, nobody in her village would lock the front doors and the place used to feel safe, but last month she met a neighbour in the street when she was walking home from the shops and heard some bad news. Thieves were breaking into people's houses while they were sitting in their back gardens.

She realised that she didn't want to live alone any more. She isn't used to being in the town yet, but it's not as difficult as she thought it might be, and she loves seeing us more often.

1. refused — Refused to + infinitivo: se negaba a cambiar de casa.

2. changed — Acción puntual el mes pasado.

3. asked — Pregunta en un momento pasado.

4. would lock — Costumbre repetida: nadie cerraba con llave.

5. used to feel — Estado pasado: el pueblo antes parecía seguro.

6. met — Encuentro puntual.

7. was walking — Estaba regresando de las tiendas.

8. heard — Oyó una noticia.

9. were breaking — Robos que ocurrían en aquel periodo.

10. were sitting — Acción simultánea a los robos.

11. realised — Se dio cuenta.

12. didn't want — Want es normalmente estativo, así que Past Simple.

13. isn't used — Aún no está acostumbrada.

14. thought — Lo que había pensado antes.

4. Be used to / Get used to

1. Rita's very tired this morning. She ___ (not) going to bed late.

Respuesta: isn't used to

No está acostumbrada al hábito de trasnochar.

2. Don't worry about the children: they ___ going to school by bus.

Respuesta: are used to

Ya están familiarizados con el autobús.

3. My new boss ___ giving orders, not receiving them.

Respuesta: is used to

Costumbre actual.

4. She ___ (not) drinking very strong coffee and it made her ill.

Respuesta: wasn't used to

No estaba acostumbrada en el pasado.

5. ___ (you) our climate or do you still miss the sunshine?

Respuesta: Are you getting used to

Proceso actual de adaptación.

6. I had never stayed in such an expensive hotel before, but I soon ___ it.

Respuesta: got used to

Adaptación completada en el pasado.

5. Cambridge First: Past Simple or Past Continuous

1. Don't ask me about the concert! I ___ (work) in the stadium café when the band ___ (play).

Respuesta: was working / was playing

Acciones simultáneas en progreso.

2. I ___ (look) round the palace when a man ___ (stop) me to ask for directions.

Respuesta: was looking / stopped

Acción de fondo y evento puntual.

3. The lights ___ (go) out while she ___ (have) a party in her house.

Respuesta: went / was having

Interrupción y evento en desarrollo.

4. She ___ (go) quietly out of her bedroom and ___ (stand) behind the door so she could hear what they were saying.

Respuesta: went / stood

Secuencia de acciones terminadas.

5. I ___ (hope) my colleagues would say 'happy birthday' when I got to work, but nobody ___ (say) anything.

Respuesta: was hoping / said

Expectativa en curso y resultado puntual.

6. Correct the mistakes with used to

1. When I was a child I am used to go camping.

Respuesta: When I was a child, I used to go camping.

Hábito pasado, no familiaridad actual.

2. My parents used to often take me to the zoo.

Respuesta: My parents often used to take me to the zoo.

Orden más natural de often; la versión original es comprensible.

3. They didn't used to help their parents with housework, but now they do.

Respuesta: They didn't use to help their parents with housework, but now they do.

Después de did se usa use sin -d.

4. We use to go to the beach every day last summer.

Respuesta: We went to the beach every day last summer.

Past Simple para un periodo específico terminado: last summer.

5. Helen is a teacher at a primary school, so she used to teach children.

Respuesta: Helen is a teacher at a primary school, so she teaches children.

El presente indica una actividad actual.

6. I would like to be a sports instructor because I was used to train tennis players before I came to England.

Respuesta: I would like to be a sports instructor because I used to train tennis players before I came to England.

Used to train = antiguo hábito; was used to training = estaba acostumbrado a entrenar.

Resumen de reglas

FormaFunción
Past SimpleAcción terminada o secuencia.
Past ContinuousSituación en progreso en un momento pasado.
Used to + infinitivoCostumbre o estado del pasado.
Would + infinitivoAcción habitual repetida.
Be used to + nombre/-ingEstar acostumbrado.
Get used to + nombre/-ingAcostumbrarse progresivamente.
