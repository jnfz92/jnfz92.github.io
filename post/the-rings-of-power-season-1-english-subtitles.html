5. Common mistakes for Spanish speakers

Do not try to understand every word

Fantasy stories contain names, invented terms and formal vocabulary. First identify the action, the speaker's intention and the key verbs.

Do not keep Spanish subtitles on during the listening stage

If your goal is listening practice, switch to English [CC]. Spanish subtitles are useful for checking meaning, but they can make you read Spanish instead of listening to English.

Closed captions include sound information

English [CC] can include descriptions such as music or sound effects. Treat those descriptions as extra vocabulary rather than dialogue you need to memorize.

  2. ally
  3. threat
  4. realm

a) a person who supports you · b) a serious promise · c) a possible danger · d) a kingdom or territory

Answers: 1-b, 2-a, 3-c, 4-d.

B. Mini challenge.

Watch five minutes of any Season 1 episode with English [CC]. Write down three verbs you hear, then create a new sentence with each verb. Replay the same five minutes without subtitles and see whether you can recognize them.
