Parte 1. Ejercicios sin resolver

3. Choose the correct form of the verbs in this text

My granny is 93 and she's come to live with us at our house. We're all pleased because we love having her near us. She's a very independent person and until this year, she (1) refused / was refusing to move to the flat on our ground floor. But last month she suddenly (2) changed / was changing her mind and I (3) asked / would ask her why. She explained that for years, nobody in her village (4) would lock / was locking their front doors and the place (5) used to feel / would feel safe, but last month (6) she met / was meeting a neighbour in the street when she (7) was walking / would walk home from the shops and (8) heard / was hearing some bad news. Thieves (9) were breaking / got used to breaking into people's houses while they (10) were sitting / would sit in their back gardens.

She (11) realised / was realising that she (12) wasn't wanting / didn't want to live alone any more. She (13) isn't used / didn't use to being in the town yet, but it's not as difficult as she (14) was thinking / thought it might be, and she loves seeing us more often.

4. Fill in the gaps with a suitable form of be/get used to

  1. Rita's very tired this morning. She ______ (not) going to bed late.
  2. Don't worry about the children: they ______ going to school by bus.
  3. My new boss ______ giving orders, not receiving them.
  4. She ______ (not) drinking very strong coffee and it made her ill.
  5. ______ (you) our climate or do you still miss the sunshine?
  6. I had never stayed in such an expensive hotel before, but I soon ______ it.

5. Complete with Past Simple or Past Continuous

  1. Don't ask me about the concert! I ______ (work) in the stadium café when the band ______ (play).
  2. I ______ (look) round the palace when a man ______ (stop) me to ask for directions.
  3. The lights ______ (go) out while she ______ (have) a party in her house.
  4. She ______ (go) quietly out of her bedroom and ______ (stand) behind the door so she could hear what they were saying.
  5. I ______ (hope) my colleagues would say 'happy birthday' when I got to work, but nobody ______ (say) anything.

6. Correct the mistakes with used to

  1. When I was a child I am used to go camping.
  2. My parents used to often take me to the zoo.
  3. They didn't used to help their parents with housework, but now they do.
  4. We use to go to the beach every day last summer.
  5. Helen is a teacher at a primary school, so she used to teach children.
  6. I would like to be a sports instructor because I was used to train tennis players before I came to England.

Parte 2. Respuestas explicadas

3. Texto completo resuelto

My granny is 93 and she's come to live with us at our house. We're all pleased because we love having her near us. She's a very independent person and until this year, she (1) refused to move to the flat on our ground floor. But last month she suddenly (2) changed her mind and I (3) asked her why. She explained that for years, nobody in her village (4) would lock their front doors and the place (5) used to feel safe, but last month (6) she met a neighbour in the street when she (7) was walking home from the shops and (8) heard some bad news. Thieves (9) were breaking into people's houses while they (10) were sitting in their back gardens.

She (11) realised that she (12) didn't want to live alone any more. She (13) isn't used to being in the town yet, but it's not as difficult as she (14) thought it might be, and she loves seeing us more often.

Por qué se elige cada verbo

  1. refused

    Una negativa o situación pasada ya acabada; Past Simple.

  2. changed

    «Last month» y «suddenly» indican un cambio puntual.

  3. asked

    Preguntó una vez, no era un hábito repetido.

  4. would lock

    Con «for years» expresa la costumbre de cerrar con llave; con «nobody», la ausencia de esa costumbre.

  5. used to feel

    Feel describe un estado habitual anterior; «would» no suele expresar este tipo de estado.

  6. met

    Se encontró con una persona en un momento concreto.

  7. was walking

    Estaba caminando cuando ocurrió el encuentro.

  8. heard

    Oyó una noticia: hecho puntual.

  9. were breaking

    Los robos estaban ocurriendo durante ese periodo.

  10. were sitting

    Acción simultánea en curso: estaban sentados en el jardín.

  11. realised

    «Realise» se utiliza normalmente como verbo de estado o percepción, aquí cambio puntual.

  12. didn't want

    «Want» es normalmente estativo; Past Simple negativo.

  13. isn't used

    Be used to + -ing significa estar acostumbrado, y «yet» indica que todavía no lo está.

  14. thought

    Describe lo que había pensado, no un proceso en desarrollo.

4. Be / Get used to: soluciones

  1. isn't used to

    No está acostumbrada a acostarse tarde; be used to + -ing.

  2. are used to

    Los niños ya están acostumbrados a ir en autobús.

  3. is used to

    Es su costumbre actual: dar órdenes.

  4. wasn't used to

    En aquel momento no estaba acostumbrada al café fuerte.

  5. Are you getting used to

    Pregunta por el proceso de adaptación que sigue en curso.

  6. got used to

    Se acostumbró al hotel en el pasado.

5. Past Simple / Past Continuous: soluciones

  1. was working / was playing

    Ambas acciones estaban ocurriendo a la vez.

  2. was looking / stopped

    Acción de fondo interrumpida por un hecho puntual.

  3. went / was having

    La luz se apagó durante la fiesta.

  4. went / stood

    Dos acciones sucesivas y terminadas.

  5. was hoping / said

    Una expectativa en curso frente a un resultado puntual; «hoped» también puede ser posible.

6. Used to: frases corregidas

  1. When I was a child, I used to go camping.

    Used to + infinitivo: costumbre del pasado.

  2. My parents often used to take me to the zoo.

    «Often used to» es el orden más habitual; «used to often» también se oye, aunque el libro pide corregirlo.

  3. They didn't use to help their parents with housework, but now they do.

    Después de «didn't» se emplea «use to», sin -d.

  4. We went to the beach every day last summer.

    «Last summer» delimita un periodo concreto terminado; aquí el Past Simple es la opción natural.

  5. Helen is a teacher at a primary school, so she teaches children.

    Enseña actualmente: Present Simple, no «used to».

  6. I would like to be a sports instructor because I used to train tennis players before I came to England.

    «Used to train» indica hábito pasado; «was used to training» indicaría estar acostumbrado.
