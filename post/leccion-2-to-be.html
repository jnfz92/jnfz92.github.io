Lesson 02

The Verb “To Be”

Master am, is, and are in affirmative sentences, negatives, questions, and everyday situations.

Beginner A1 · Detailed guide

1. The three present forms

SubjectFormExample
IamI am ready.
he / she / itisShe is here.
you / we / theyareThey are busy.

2. Contractions

In everyday English, contractions are very common: I'm, you're, he's, she's, it's, we're, they're.

3. Negative sentences

Put not after the verb: I am not tired. She isn't late. We aren't ready.

4. Questions

Move the verb before the subject: Are you ready? Is she at home? Am I late?

5. Essential uses

  • Identity: I'm Marta.
  • Profession: I'm a designer.
  • Origin: He is from Rome.
  • Feelings: We are hungry.
  • Location: The keys are on the table.
  • Age: I'm 25.
Common mistake: English uses be for age. Say “I am 25,” not “I have 25.”

6. Practice

Choose am, is, or are:
  1. I ___ ready.
  2. She ___ from Canada.
  3. We ___ friends.
  4. They ___ not at home.
  5. ___ you tired?

Answers: am · is · are · are · Are
