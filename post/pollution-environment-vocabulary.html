Palabras clave

EnglishEspañolEjemplo
emissionsemisionesVehicle emissions affect air quality.
wasteresiduosWe should reduce waste.
litterbasura tiradaDon't leave litter on the beach.
sewageaguas residualesSewage can contaminate rivers.
landfillvertederoToo much waste ends up in landfill.
recyclingreciclajeRecycling saves resources.
renewable energyenergía renovableRenewable energy can reduce emissions.
environmentmedio ambienteWe must protect the environment.

Verbos útiles

reduce, reuse, recycle, protect, prevent, contaminate, pollute, emit, dump, clean up.

Pollution es incontable: di pollution is a problem, no “pollutions are”.
