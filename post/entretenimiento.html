TV & Movies

Learn English with TV Shows and Movies

Train your listening, vocabulary, and pronunciation with short scenes, subtitles, and a simple weekly routine.

Beginner A1–A2 · Detailed guide

1. A simple four-step method

  1. Watch for context. — Observa el contexto. Fíjate en quién habla, dónde están y qué está ocurriendo.
  2. Replay with English subtitles. — Vuelve a verlo con subtítulos en inglés. Anota entre 3 y 5 expresiones útiles.
  3. Listen without reading. — Escucha sin leer. Intenta reconocer las expresiones que has estudiado.
  4. Imitate one sentence. — Imita una frase. Copia el ritmo y la entonación.
Realistic goal: learning one reusable phrase is more valuable than translating every word in a long scene.

2. What works well for beginners?

Choose shows with everyday conversations, short episodes, and clear visual situations such as family life, friendships, school, work, restaurants, or travel.

3. How to choose movies

Stories you already know, animation, and family movies can be easier because the context lets you focus on the language. Avoid highly technical dialogue at first.

4. How to use subtitles

StageSubtitlesGoal
1Your strongest languageUnderstand context
2EnglishConnect sound and spelling
3NoneTrain listening

5. Phrases to listen for

  • What's going on? — ¿Qué está pasando?
  • Are you okay? — ¿Estás bien?
  • I don't know. — No lo sé.
  • Come on! — ¡Vamos!
  • Wait a minute. — Espera un momento.
  • What do you mean? — ¿Qué quieres decir?
  • That's great! — ¡Eso es genial!
  • See you later. — Hasta luego.
