Outstanding People: Photo Description & Discussion

Modelo de respuestas B2 First para una foto de un joven aficionado haciéndose un selfie con un futbolista en un estadio.

Publicado el 6 de octubre de 2026

Objetivo: responder de forma natural, desarrollar ideas y usar lenguaje de especulación, descripción y opinión propio de un nivel B2.

A. Look at the picture and answer the questions

1. Who do you think the people taking a selfie are? Where are they?

I think one of them is a professional football player and the other one is a young fan. They are probably at a football stadium, near the pitch, just after or before a match.

Useful language: I think..., probably..., near the pitch...

2. What are the people around them doing?

The people around them are watching, taking photos and trying to get closer to the player. Some of them seem excited because they may also want a selfie or an autograph.

Useful language: seem excited, may also want...

3. What do you think they have just said to each other? What’s going to happen next?

The fan has probably just asked, “Can I take a selfie with you?” and the player has agreed. Next, they’re going to take the photo, and the fan will probably thank him before leaving.

Grammar focus: Present Perfect + just, going to and will probably.

B. Discuss the questions

1. On what occasions do you normally take photos?

I normally take photos when I’m travelling, when I spend time with friends or family, and on special occasions such as birthdays or celebrations. I also take pictures when I see something unusual or beautiful that I want to remember.

2. If you could take a selfie with a famous person, who would you choose and why?

If I could take a selfie with a famous person, I would probably choose a successful athlete. I admire people who are disciplined and work hard to achieve their goals. I think it would be interesting to meet someone who performs at such a high level.

B2 structure: second conditional + reason + personal opinion.

3. What role do you think famous people play in society? Should they be good role models? Should they inspire other people?

I think famous people can have a strong influence on society, especially on young people. In my opinion, they should try to be good role models because many people follow what they do and say. However, I don’t think they have to be perfect. They are still human beings and can make mistakes. The most important thing is that they use their influence in a responsible way and, if possible, inspire people to do something positive.

Useful connector: However... para introducir contraste y desarrollar una respuesta más equilibrada.

Useful B2 expressions for this type of task

  • I think / I’d say... — para dar una opinión.
  • They seem to be... — para describir sin afirmar demasiado.
  • They might / may be... — para especular.
  • It looks as if... — para interpretar una escena.
  • In my opinion... — para desarrollar una idea.
  • However... — para contrastar.
  • If I could..., I would... — para respuestas hipotéticas.
