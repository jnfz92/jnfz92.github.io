Despite vs. In Spite Of: diferencias y ejercicios

Cuándo usar despite, in spite of, although y por qué despite of es incorrecto, con transformaciones de Cambridge B2.

9 de octubre de 2026

Despite e in spite of significan «a pesar de». Despite of es incorrecto. La diferencia principal es de estructura, no de significado.

CorrectoIncorrecto
Despite the rainDespite of the rain
In spite of the rainInspite of the rain
Despite being tiredDespite of being tired

1. Seguidos de sustantivo

Despite the rain, we went for a walk.A pesar de la lluvia, salimos a pasear.
In spite of her problems, she remained optimistic.A pesar de sus problemas, siguió siendo optimista.

2. Seguidos de -ing

Despite feeling tired, I went to work.A pesar de estar cansado, fui a trabajar.
In spite of studying hard, he failed the exam.A pesar de estudiar mucho, suspendió el examen.

3. Oración completa: the fact that

Utiliza despite the fact that o in spite of the fact that antes de sujeto + verbo.

Despite the fact that it was raining, we went outside.A pesar de que llovía, salimos.

4. Although vs. despite

Although + oraciónDespite / in spite of + nombre o -ing
Although he was tiredDespite being tired
Although it was rainingDespite the rain
Although she was nervousIn spite of being nervous

5. Errores frecuentes

❌ Despite of the rain → ✅ Despite the rain
❌ Despite she was ill → ✅ Despite being ill / Despite the fact that she was ill
❌ In spite of he was tired → ✅ In spite of being tired.

6. Transformaciones B2

  1. Although it was cold, we went swimming. (DESPITE)
  2. Although she was nervous, she gave a speech. (IN SPITE OF)
  3. Despite the rain, they played tennis. (ALTHOUGH)
  4. He passed the exam although he hadn't studied much. (DESPITE)
Mostrar soluciones
  1. Despite the cold, we went swimming.
  2. In spite of being nervous, she gave a speech.
  3. Although it was raining, they played tennis.
  4. Despite not having studied much, he passed the exam.
