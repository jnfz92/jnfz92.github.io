Lesson 01

Greetings and Introductions

Learn the essential greetings and introduction patterns you need for your first English conversations.

Beginner A1 · Detailed guide

1. Basic greetings

Hello! — ¡Hola! A neutral greeting for most situations.
Hi! — ¡Hola! A friendly, informal greeting.
Good morning. — Buenos días. Use it in the morning.
Good afternoon. — Buenas tardes. Use it after midday.
Good evening. — Buenas tardes/noches. Use it when greeting someone in the evening.

2. Introduce yourself

The simplest patterns are I'm + name and My name is + name.

Hi, I'm Alex. — Hola, soy Alex.
Hello, my name is Sara. — Hola, me llamo Sara.

3. Ask someone's name

Use What's your name? — ¿Cómo te llamas? In a more formal situation, you can say May I ask your name? — ¿Puedo preguntarte tu nombre?

4. Ask how someone is

How are you? — I'm fine, thanks.
¿Cómo estás? — Estoy bien, gracias.
How's it going? — Pretty good!
¿Qué tal? — ¡Bastante bien!
Tip: “How's it going?” is more informal than “How are you?”

5. Say goodbye

  • Goodbye. — Adiós. Neutral.
  • Bye! — ¡Adiós! Common and informal.
  • See you later. — Hasta luego. You expect to meet again.
  • Have a nice day! — ¡Que tengas un buen día! A friendly closing.

6. Practice

Complete the mini-dialogue:

A: Hello! My ___ is Emma. — ¡Hola! Mi ___ es Emma.
B: Hi Emma. ___ are you? — Hola, Emma. ¿___ estás?
A: I'm fine, thanks. See you ___! — Estoy bien, gracias. ¡Nos vemos ___!

Answers: name · How · later
