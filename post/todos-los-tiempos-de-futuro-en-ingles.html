Las principales formas de futuro

FormaUso principalEjemplo
will + infinitiveDecisiones espontáneas, promesas y prediccionesI’ll help you.
be going to + infinitiveIntenciones y predicciones con evidenciaI’m going to study tonight.
Present ContinuousPlanes ya organizadosI’m meeting Ana at six.
Present SimpleHorarios y programasThe train leaves at eight.
Future ContinuousAcción en progreso en un momento futuroI’ll be working at ten.
Future PerfectAcción terminada antes de un punto futuroI’ll have finished by Friday.
Future Perfect ContinuousDuración hasta un punto futuroI’ll have been working here for ten years.

1. Will

Usamos will + infinitive para decisiones tomadas en el momento de hablar, promesas, ofrecimientos y predicciones basadas principalmente en opinión.

  • Decisión espontánea: The phone is ringing. I’ll answer it.
  • Promesa: I’ll call you tonight.
  • Predicción: I think it will be a good year.
Forma negativa: will not = won't.

2. Be going to

Usamos be going to + infinitive para intenciones o planes y para predicciones cuando vemos evidencia presente.

I’m going to start a new course. → Tengo la intención de empezar un curso.
Look at those clouds! It’s going to rain. → Hay evidencia de lluvia.

3. Present Continuous para futuro

Se usa para planes futuros que ya están organizados, normalmente con una hora, lugar o persona concreta.

I’m meeting my teacher tomorrow at 5.
We’re flying to Rome on Friday.
Comparación: I’m going to visit London expresa intención; I’m flying to London on Friday at 9 suena más organizado.

4. Present Simple para horarios

Usamos Present Simple para horarios oficiales, programas, calendarios y eventos programados.

The train leaves at 8:15.
The lesson starts at nine.

5. Future Continuous

Se forma con will be + verb-ing. Describe una actividad que estará en progreso en un momento futuro.

This time tomorrow, I’ll be travelling to Madrid.
Mañana a esta hora estaré viajando a Madrid.

También puede usarse para preguntar educadamente por planes:

Will you be using the car tonight?

6. Future Perfect

Se forma con will have + past participle y expresa que una acción estará terminada antes de un momento futuro.

By Friday, I’ll have finished the report.
Para el viernes, habré terminado el informe.
Marcadores frecuentes: by, by then, by the time, before.

7. Future Perfect Continuous

Se forma con will have been + verb-ing y destaca la duración de una actividad hasta un punto futuro.

By next June, I’ll have been studying English for three years.
Para el próximo junio, llevaré tres años estudiando inglés.

Errores frecuentes

  • ❌ I will to call you. ✅ I will call you.
  • ❌ I going to study. ✅ I’m going to study.
  • ❌ I’m meet him tomorrow. ✅ I’m meeting him tomorrow.
  • ❌ By Friday I will finish already. ✅ By Friday, I’ll have finished.

Ejercicios

  1. The phone is ringing. I ________ (answer) it.
  2. Look at those clouds! It ________ (rain).
  3. I ________ (meet) Laura tomorrow at 6; we arranged it yesterday.
  4. The train ________ (leave) at 07:30.
  5. This time tomorrow, we ________ (fly) to New York.
  6. By next week, she ________ (finish) the project.
  7. By 2030, he ________ (work) here for twenty years.
  8. I think people ________ (use) more renewable energy in the future.

Soluciones

  1. will answer
  2. is going to rain
  3. am meeting
  4. leaves
  5. will be flying
  6. will have finished
  7. will have been working
  8. will use
